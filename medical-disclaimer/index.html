Medical disclaimer

The information on GallbladderPain.org is for general information only. It is not medical advice.

Our guides are written to help you understand how food choices may affect gallstone symptoms. They can't take account of your personal medical history, other conditions or medicines. Always follow the advice of your GP, specialist, dietitian or pharmacist, and speak to them before making big changes to your diet or taking supplements.

If you have severe tummy pain, pain spreading to your back with vomiting, a high temperature, or yellow skin or eyes, call 999 or go to A&E. For pain lasting longer than 30 minutes, get urgent advice from your GP or NHS 111.
