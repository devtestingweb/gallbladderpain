About GallbladderPain.org

GallbladderPain.org helps people living with gallstones understand their gallbladder pain and eat in a way that makes attacks less likely.

Gallstones are very common in the UK, and many people wait months between diagnosis and treatment. In that time, the advice they get about food is often a single line: "avoid fatty food". This site fills in the detail: what the pain means, when to get help, and what to eat, one food at a time.

How we write our guides

  • We check against trusted sources. Our main references are NHS guidance, NHS hospital dietetic advice and published research. Every guide lists its sources.
  • We say when evidence is weak. If something is based on small or old studies, we tell you.
  • We don't promise cures. No food, juice or cleanse has been shown to dissolve gallstones, and we won't pretend otherwise.
  • We keep pages up to date. Each page shows when it was last updated.

How the site is funded
